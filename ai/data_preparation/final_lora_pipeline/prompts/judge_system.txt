- key_points: exactly 4 short high-level sentences

Judge semantic quality strictly:
- unsupported additions are not acceptable
- critical omissions are not acceptable
- negation, uncertainty, modality, warnings, requirements, restrictions, eligibility rules, and major conclusions must be preserved when important
- key points should summarize major semantic units, not list every local step or minor detail

Return exactly one JSON object using the required judge schema. Do not include markdown or extra text.
